## 📖 Overview

UltraSwitch is a service that simplifies the process of transferring data from one device to another. Whether you are switching devices or just need a backup, UltraSwitch supports multiple operating systems including Android, iOS, Linux, MacOS, Windows, BSD, and Haiku.

## 📚 System Requirements

Before installing UltraSwitch, make sure your device meets the following requirements:

- **Windows**: Windows 10 or newer.
- **MacOS**: macOS Mojave (10.14) or newer.
- **Linux**: Any recent version of your preferred distribution.
- **Android**: Android 5.0 (Lollipop) or newer.
- **iOS**: iOS 10 or newer.

## 🔧 Features

UltraSwitch offers a variety of features to make data transfer and backup simple and effective:

- **Cross-Platform Compatibility**: Transfer data between different operating systems without hassle.
- **Full Backups**: Backup everything from contacts to apps, making switching devices easy.
- **User-Friendly Interface**: Designed for everyone, even those without technical skills.
- **Secure Transfers**: Keep your data safe during transfer with strong encryption.
